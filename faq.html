Getting Started

Who is Stardom designed for?

Stardom works with builders, developers, owners and investors who want a coordinated housing material package. The starting point is your project scope, drawings and delivery requirements.

How are the building systems organised?

Builder packages are organised into nine material categories: Light Gauge Steel Framing, Roof & External Cladding, Windows & Doors, Insulation & Linings, Flooring, Kitchen, Bathroom, Plumbing & Pipework, and Patio & Outdoor Living. Supply-chain coordination is explained separately under How We Deliver. The detailed technical sections support these package categories; the written quotation defines the actual inclusions.

Can I enquire about a single system?

Yes. You can discuss one or more selected systems, a complete-home package or a repeatable multi-project program. The final supply scope is confirmed in the project quotation.

Can I start with my existing house design?

Yes. Share the architectural, structural and engineering information you already have. The team can discuss the schedules, supplier details and interface reviews needed to prepare a coordinated material package.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Include your project location, intended use, available drawing revision, home or system selection, material scope and expected timing. You can describe which drawings you have in the enquiry and share them with the team by email.

Design & Products

How are house models and product options selected?

The house model, layout and material package are reviewed against your brief and available project information. Catalogue models and options are starting points; final geometry, availability and inclusions are confirmed for the named project.

Can products be ordered before construction is complete?

Many items can be coordinated from sufficiently detailed drawings. Release to production needs agreed dimensions, interface details and reviewed supplier shop drawings. Products that depend on finished site measurements need a separate measurement and release plan.

How are window and door dimensions coordinated?

The opening schedule is reviewed with frame sizes, rough openings, reveals, sill levels, hardware and flashing details. These values need to agree with the wall assembly and supplier shop drawings before production.

How are kitchens and bathrooms coordinated?

Joinery layouts are connected to room dimensions, appliance clearances, sink and tap locations, plumbing rough-ins and finish levels. Bathroom selections also need coordinated fixture dimensions, wastes, falls and waterproofing interfaces.

Can I customise finishes and storage?

The catalogues provide finish, cabinet, hardware and storage directions to discuss. Options are reviewed for the selected design and supply scope, then recorded in the project schedule and approved supplier drawings.

Are catalogue dimensions locked production specifications?

No. The catalogue includes design references and target selections. The approved project schedule, drawing revision, selected product evidence and supplier shop drawings establish the final values for production.

Are blinds and curtains made from plan dimensions?

The Blinds & Curtains collection uses final site measurements. Plans help select the intended fabric, control and mounting direction, while the measured opening and installation conditions establish the order dimensions.

Supply & Delivery

What does Stardom China Hub coordinate?

The China Hub supports product specifications, supplier communications, manufacturing details, quality records, packing and shipment coordination. Its role is to connect the selected materials to the same project information.

Must every supplier be based in China?

The project can be discussed with products sourced from China or other markets. Supplier selection is based on the required specification, supporting product information, quality, availability and the agreed delivery scope.

What is a bill of materials?

A bill of materials, or BOM, lists the selected products and quantities for the project. Stardom's system connects the BOM to product/SKU references, drawing revisions, supplier records, purchase orders and packing information.

How are changes managed after selection?

Changes to a product, quantity or drawing need to be reviewed against the affected interfaces. The agreed change is recorded against the project schedule and BOM before the relevant order or production release is updated.

What happens before a shipment is released?

The relevant product specifications, supplier drawings, supporting evidence, quality checks and packing records are reviewed. Outstanding issues need to be resolved or addressed through the agreed release conditions.

How is the material package prepared for site delivery?

Packing information identifies the products, quantities, pack references, dimensions, weight and project allocation. The container and delivery plan is coordinated with the agreed receiving and installation sequence.

Scope & Contact

Does a material package include all local works?

The quotation needs to list the included materials and the separate local scope. Foundations, site preparation, labour, services, approvals, installation and related costs are confirmed individually rather than assumed to be included.

Who handles project design and installation?

The appointed designers, engineers, builders and relevant trades retain their project roles. The product package and supply coordination are connected to the local design, review and installation scope.

How are product documents reviewed?

Review uses the actual selected model, intended application, installation instructions and relevant supporting evidence. A catalogue statement or a different product's document does not replace the information needed for the selected project.

How are price and lead time confirmed?

Price and timing are confirmed against the agreed project scope, product choices, quantities, drawing information, supplier availability and delivery plan. Contact the team for a project-specific quotation.
