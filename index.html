Your Questions, Answered

A practical starting point for your Stardom project.

Who does Stardom work with?

Builders, developers, investors and owners looking for a coordinated housing material package.

Can I start with my existing drawings?

Yes. Share the available architectural, structural and engineering drawings. The team can discuss the additional schedules and supplier details required before production.

Can materials be ordered before the house is built?

Many products can be coordinated from sufficiently detailed drawings. Production needs agreed dimensions, interface details and reviewed supplier shop drawings. Items dependent on finished site measurements need their own release plan.

What is included in a Stardom package?

The scope is project-specific. It may include structure, roofing, external walls, windows and doors, cabinetry, bathrooms and interior finishes. Local labour, foundations, services and approvals must be listed separately in the agreed scope.

How are local requirements handled?

Your builder and relevant consultants review the project requirements and supporting product documentation. Compliance depends on the selected product, application, installation and site conditions.

How do I get a price for my project?

Start with an enquiry outlining your site, intended home, drawings and material scope. Pricing and lead times are confirmed against the agreed package.
